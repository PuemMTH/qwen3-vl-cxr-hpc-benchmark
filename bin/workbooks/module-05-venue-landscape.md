- `High`: 8–10, no critical gate
- `Medium`: 5–7 or clearly fixable gaps
- `Low`: 0–4 or any out-of-scope, major evidence/design, compliance, deadline, or resource blocker

Confidence must be lowered when official/reliable selectivity evidence is unavailable.
